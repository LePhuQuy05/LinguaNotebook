# 0003 — The SRS deck is recall-only (flashcard and grammar)

Lesson items become SRSCards only when they are recall-able units — vocabulary term/definition (flashcard) and grammar pattern/fill. Reading and listening items are comprehension exercises and deliberately do **not** enter the deck: their practice is the lesson itself, and mixing comprehension into the review stream inflates the daily review load and blurs the recall signal. This is a deliberate no — a future change that adds reading/listening cards should revisit this decision rather than treat it as an omission.
